# 🚗⚡ Meet the G700 App Ecosystem: Next-Level In-Car Intelligence

Transform your driving experience with the **G700 App Family**—the ultimate, privacy-first ecosystem designed specifically for modern Android car head units and smartphones. Built for performance, security, and seamless vehicle integration, here is a professional overview of what makes the G700 ecosystem a true game-changer:

---

### 🌟 **Car Android G700: The Ultimate Head-Unit Powerhouse**
Engineered exclusively for Android 11+ car displays, this application brings robust control right to your dashboard with **zero ads, zero tracking, and absolute privacy**.
* **Smart Floating Action Button & App Grid:** Access your frequently used apps instantly, search through categorized chips, and clean up background memory with a single tap via an integrated Accessibility Service.
* **Rear Passenger Entertainment (Display 5):** Mirror your main screen onto the rear passenger display or launch independent applications seamlessly, supported by local ADB touch-forwarding.
* **Intelligent Vehicle Integration & Automated Locks:** Enjoy advanced automation like customizable Delayed Lock sequences (flashing hazards, folding mirrors, shutting down screens), automatic state restoration, and Fob-Lock Assist to secure your car when leaving it running.
* **Secure Over-The-Air Updates:** Background checks occur every 6 hours, utilizing HTTPS and cryptographic SHA-256 verification to ensure your system updates safely without manual hassle.

### 📱 **G700 RemoteZ: Your Car, Right in Your Pocket**
Take total control of your vehicle straight from your smartphone. 
* **BLE-Powered Companion:** Pair your phone securely via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) with local fingerprint verification.
* **Remote Control at Your Fingertips:** Read live vehicle metrics, manage rear-screen mirroring, launch permitted apps, and lock or unlock your doors on demand.

### 🔒 **Frictionless & Secure Distribution Hub**
* **Streamlined Releases:** Every signed APK (`car-v<version>` and `remote-v<version>`) is centrally distributed through GitHub via dedicated `*-latest` pointers and lightweight `update.json` files.
* **Token-Free Accessibility:** Public repositories allow in-app auto-updaters and web installers to fetch new builds securely over HTTPS without requiring user authentication tokens.
